# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + vanilla JavaScript + GSAP (confirmed by the user, Oct 2026). No framework.

## Users

- Primary: adults in Metro Manila considering cosmetic surgery, non-surgical aesthetic treatments or information on stem-cell interventions. They arrive curious and often anxious, want to understand options before talking to anyone, and need a low-pressure way to request a consultation.
- Evaluator: the owner or marketing lead of one specific clinic that heynuff is pitching. This demo is a sales piece: it must show them their own site, done better than their competitors'.

## Product Purpose

A pitch demonstration of a website for one aesthetic and wellness clinic. Visitors should understand what the clinic offers across three areas (surgical, non-surgical, stem-cell information), trust the people and the process, and start a consultation request. Success for the pitch: the prospect clinic sees itself in it and wants it built.

## Positioning

One clinic that covers surgery, skin and injectable treatments, and regenerative-medicine education under one consultation-first process. Education and honest suitability come before selling a procedure.

## Operating Context

- Metro Manila, two locations in the current content: Makati (Ayala Avenue) and Bonifacio Global City. Hours Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Patients browse mostly on phones, often from social media links.
- The consultation request has three steps: interest, details (In clinic or Video call, contact details and preferred day/time; clinic only for in-clinic requests), and review with local Send/Done preview. Video consultation is a demo preference, not a calling service or verified availability. No booking backend; nothing is sent or stored.

- Visit explains both formats through a four-step switchable walkthrough. Articles starts with one sourced preparation guide; it has honest demo authorship and no clinician-review claim. Real clinic operations and clinical copy need approval before launch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Treatment content (from the client brief): 10 surgical procedures grouped Face / Breast / Body; non-surgical NCTF, PRX, Rejuran, biostimulators, Botox, fillers, Ultra V threads, Aqualyx, Lemon Bottle grouped Skin / Injectables / Contour; stem-cell education in three topics (autologous cells, evidence, questions to ask).
- Medical copy is general education. No efficacy, safety or regulatory-approval claims for any product or stem-cell intervention.
- Open: the prospect clinic's real name, logo, staff, prices and photos are not yet supplied. "Aurelis" is the working name.

## Brand Commitments

- Working name: Aurelis, Aesthetic & Wellness Clinic.
- Existing content and information architecture are kept; the previous visual world (cream, brass, espresso, Cormorant serif) is not binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real testimonials, patient results, before/after photos, prices, clinician credentials or hospital affiliations. None may be fabricated.
- Clinicians and photography are AI-generated illustrations and must stay labelled as illustrative where a visitor could mistake them for real.
- Existing generated photography: assets/originals (hero portrait, clinic interior, three clinicians, two beauty portraits, glass-sphere still life).

## Product Principles

1. Understanding before booking: every treatment is explained in plain language, with its limits.
2. Honest by default: no invented results, credentials or claims.
3. One clear next step: arranging a consultation is always within reach, never pushed.
4. Built for the phone first; the desktop experience is the showpiece for the pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text readable on phones (no microscopic labels), WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard and reduced-motion support.
